## Strategic implication

No Amazon presence means no Amazon "Customers say" aggregation, no Amazon Q&A thread, and no Amazon review distribution to mine for objections — the *only* current public review surface for this brand is its own site/Thingtesting (see `hero-reviews-trustpilot.md`). This is a single-point-of-failure for social proof: if a prospective customer wants a second opinion beyond the brand's own curated testimonials, there is currently nowhere obvious for them to look. This is itself a research gap worth closing (see main report Section 4.3) and a legitimate reason to prioritize UGC/testimonial volume before scaling paid spend.
