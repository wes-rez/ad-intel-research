## Themes

- **Shining feedback:** fast, cheap, available everywhere (retail ubiquity itself is the praised feature).

## Limitations

Rating distributions are retailer-reported aggregates, not independently verified; the single side-effect report should not be generalized. No Trustpilot profile was found for AZO specifically — likely because it is a legacy retail-pharmacy brand rather than a DTC brand built around a Trustpilot-style review program.
