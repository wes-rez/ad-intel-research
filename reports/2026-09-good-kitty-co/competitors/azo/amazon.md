## Finding

**58,608 ratings at 4.7/5 is, by a wide margin, the single largest review-volume data point found across this entire research run** — larger than Uqora's Amazon presence, Uqora's Trustpilot presence, and Walmart's AZO review count combined. This is the clearest quantified evidence of AZO's retail-scale distribution advantage: it is not winning on story or design, it is winning on being everywhere, cheap, and already trusted by tens of thousands of buyers who never needed to be "sold" on a brand story.

## Competitive implication

AZO is not a brand Good Kitty Co should try to out-scale on proof volume or price. The right competitive posture is differentiation (doctor-formulated specificity, design, prevention-first vs. symptom-relief-first), not a volume fight Good Kitty cannot win in the near term.
