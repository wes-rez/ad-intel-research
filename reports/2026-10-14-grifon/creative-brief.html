1 · Trust is the bottleneck, not the product

On-site reviews are strong (GS430: 51 reviews, all 5★), but Grifon has almost no independent social proof. Competitors have thousands of reviews.

2 · Competitor reviews give shoppers reasons to switch

Rust and spots on new stainless, flat bottoms that trap food, composite staining, and warranty claims that go nowhere. O

3 · An angle only Grifon can use

Reviews praise quiet and countertop match, and the parent company installs countertops. "Picked by the people who cut your countertop." I

4 · Fix the store before scaling

GS430 is listed as 16G on its page and 18G on its collection page; prices conflict between pages; template text sits next to reviews; listings show 3 addresses. R

3 authority drivers

Who the avatar trusts, and how to put them on camera.

01

Grifon fabricators and installers

Why they listen: these people cut the sink hole and see every brand succeed or fail.

Proof: countertop business "since 2004" (brand claim); Granite Grifon at 4.7★ from 177 Google reviews.

Format: installer-POV Reels, "What we see under 1,000 counters."

Real Québec homeowners
