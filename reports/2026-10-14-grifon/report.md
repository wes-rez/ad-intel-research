## Executive Summary

Grifon is the e-commerce arm of **Granite Grifon**, a countertop and renovation company in Boisbriand, QC (showroom pages for Boisbriand, Brossard and Montréal; "delivering products since 2004" per its Amazon listings). It sells undermount stainless (Eterna, Hydra Tech™ workstation, Bel Pro apron), granite composite (Azur), fireclay, faucets, and accessories, mostly **$390–$660 CAD**. Offers include free shipping over $350, a limited lifetime warranty, 30-day returns and 10% off for email sign-up.

1. **Trust, not product, is the bottleneck. [O]** Grifon has **no Trustpilot profile**, about **4 Amazon.ca ratings** across all listings, and **no community footprint** found anywhere on the open web. Kraus and Ruvati each have **2,400+ reviews on Wayfair alone** and ~1,000 on Home Depot. Grifon's on-site reviews are strong (GS430: 51 reviews at 100% 5★; AZUR30: 14 reviews, 13 at 5★), but they are vendor-hosted and unverified.
2. **Competitor VOC is full of switching triggers. [O]** Rust or spots on new stainless (Kraus, Ruvati, Kindred). Flat bottoms that pool food and water (Ruvati, Kraus). **Blanco scores 1.7/5 on Trustpilot (123 reviews, 74% 1★)**, driven by SILGRANIT staining, cracks and warranty frustration. Kindred buyers report finish wear the company "says that is not a warranty issue."
3. **Grifon owns an angle no competitor can copy. [I]** Its best reviews are about being **quiet** and **matching the countertop**, and the parent company *installs countertops*. "The sink chosen by the people who cut your countertop" is a credible authority story, backed by a local showroom and a phone number. Kraus, Ruvati and the Amazon brands are faceless by comparison.
4. **Fix site hygiene before scaling spend. [O→R]** The GS430 is listed as **16G** on its page and **18G** on its collection page. Prices conflict between pages (e.g., GS433 at $660 and also $360→$199). Stray template text sits beside reviews. Business listings show three addresses (630/644/654 Curé-Boivin), one marked closed.

## 3. Strategic Takeaways

### Opportunities
1. **"Sink + countertop, one accountable local company." [R]** No competitor can claim this. Bundle sink sales with Granite Grifon countertop jobs, and use the installers as on-camera authority.
2. **Quiet as the lead benefit. [O→R]** The pads covering 80%+ of the sink and the "much quieter than my old sink" review support a sensory hook that Kraus, Ruvati and Amazon brands don't own in reviews.
3. **Dark granite against Blanco's staining problem. [O→R]** Grifon's Azur reviewers praise black and dark finishes at ~$555–590, against Blanco at $950–1,400 with public staining complaints. Position it as "darker, quieter, half the price" (verify any staining claims before running them).
4. **Warranty-you-can-walk-into. [I→R]** Competitor VOC shows warranty claims being refused or ignored. Grifon has a showroom and a phone number. Publish plain-language warranty terms.
5. **Review-volume sprint. [R]** Goal: 100+ independent reviews in 90 days (Google/Trustpilot invites, Amazon Vine on GS430 and AZUR30, photo reviews).

### Threats
1. **Social-proof gap:** shoppers who say "reviews seem great" (Kindred buyer) will default to the brands with thousands of reviews.
2. **Amazon value tier:** the same workstation format, with best-seller badges and Prime shipping.
3. **Spec and price inconsistency** on grifon.co can kill trust at the comparison stage, where gauge is a key filter.
4. **Big-box markdown culture:** Kindred at $330 (was $805) and Ruvati at $399 USD reset what shoppers expect to pay.

### Creative Inspiration (non-Meta; evidence-derived)
1. **"Sound test"**: side-by-side, dropping a pan into an old stainless sink vs GS430 (pads) and Azur granite. Hook: "Hear the difference."
2. **"Cut by the people who cut your countertop"**: installer POV fitting an Azur sink under quartz.
3. **"The warranty desk is 20 minutes away"**: a showroom walk-through contrasted with a "your call is important to us" hold.
