### Recurring praise themes (Observed)
1. **Quiet**: thick pads and undercoating on stainless, and a dense granite composite.
2. **Countertop and faucet match**, which is the strongest design theme. It fits Grifon's countertop heritage.
3. **Included accessories**: bamboo cutting board, colander, roll-up rack, bottom grid.
4. **Depth and size.**

### Criticism and friction signals (Observed on page, not in review text)
- **Spec inconsistency:** the GS430 page says **16G**, while the workstation collection page says **18G**. Gauge is a top research criterion in this category, so this undermines trust.
- **Template residue:** stray boilerplate ("SOFT, BREEZY", "Be the first to write a review") next to real reviews, plus text from other products on some pages.
- **Price inconsistency** across product and collection pages (e.g., GS433 fireclay shows $660 and also $360 → $199; FLY faucet shows $360 and also "from $260").
- **One 1★ on AZUR30** with no text indexed, so the cause can't be determined.
