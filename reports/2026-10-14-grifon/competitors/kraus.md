**Positioning:** Mass-premium US brand (NJ) sold through Home Depot, Lowe's, Wayfair, Amazon, Walmart and Ferguson. Its Kore™ Workstation line is the category reference. 16-gauge stainless, granite (Bellucci, Quarza), limited lifetime warranty.
**Price (USD):** Kore 30" undermount workstation (KWU110-30) **$499.95** at Ferguson and AJ Madison (AJ Madison shows a $1,170 "MSRP" anchor). No CAD pricing was found.

### Shining (Observed)
- Accessories, deep basin, looks and sturdy build are the most praised themes (Wayfair summary).
- "even my contractor mentioned that this was indeed a very good sink" (Kraus Bellucci granite, Walmart, 2020).
- Offset drain, included cutting board and drying rack, thick steel (Direct Sinks reviews).

### Criticisms (Observed)
- "less than two months it started to rust" (Wayfair, Kore workstation).
- Home Depot reviewer: scratches appeared within hours of use, even with the bottom grid in place (*paraphrase*). Another reviewer said water pools because of the flat bottom.
- [Houzz](https://www.houzz.com/discussions/6249053/brand-new-kraus-core-workstation-rust-spots): "Brand new Kraus Core workstation rust spots". The owner reports "only rust spots which can off easily with abrasive side of sponge".
- Lowe's: water spots on one model.

### Warranty fine print (Observed, [Kraus warranty PDF](https://dtc.kraususa.com/media/catalog/product/documentation/kraususa-product-warranty.pdf))
Excludes "cosmetic rust, scratches or dents" from ordinary use, plus hard-water/mineral damage. Abrasive pads "can void the warranty". It covers the **original purchaser at the original location only**.

## Grifon counter-angles
- **Competitor advantage:** review volume (thousands), retail availability, brand recognition.
- **Grifon counter:** similar workstation format (GS430 at $420–490 CAD vs Kraus ~$500 USD ≈ $690 CAD), shipped from Québec, plus a local showroom where you can see the sink before buying. *Recommended, not a superiority claim.*
- **Switching signal:** the rust and scratch complaints on brand-new sinks, combined with warranty exclusions of cosmetic issues.
