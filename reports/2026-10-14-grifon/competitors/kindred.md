## Grifon counter-angles
- **Competitor advantage:** shelf presence in every Canadian big box, a "Canadian" heritage story, low sale prices.
- **Grifon counter:** both are Canadian. Grifon is **Québec-based with a showroom and a countertop install team**, offers **16G** on GS430 (vs Steel Queen 20G), and includes the accessory kit. *Verify gauge per SKU before use.*
- **Switching signal:** finish that looks worn within days, and warranty exclusions for surface wear.
