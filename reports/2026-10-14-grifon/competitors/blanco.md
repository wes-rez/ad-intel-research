# Competitor — Blanco

**Positioning:** Premium German brand. Its SILGRANIT® granite composite is the category's best-known name, and **SILGRANIT is produced in Canada** (Amazon listing: "Silgranit Production - Made in Canada"). Sold through plumbing showrooms, Home Depot.ca, Costco.ca and Faucet Express.
**Price (CAD, Canadian retailers):** PRECIS 30" undermount $562 sale / $865 reg (Rise); PRECIS U Super Single $984 sale / $1,406 reg; DIAMOND Super Single $949 sale / $1,356 reg (Faucet Express).

### Themes
- **Criticisms:** staining and discolouration (especially white/light SILGRANIT), cracks and chips, unresponsive customer service, painful warranty claims, poor value for the price.
- **Shining:** design and appearance. Occasional good warranty replacements.

## Grifon counter-angles
- **Competitor advantage:** brand prestige, designer/plumber specification, Canadian-made composite.
- **Grifon counter:** the Azur granite line at **$555–590 CAD** vs Blanco at **$950–1,400 CAD** regular in comparable undermount sizes. A local Québec company with a showroom and a phone number. Grifon's Azur reviews praise **black/dark** finishes that match the countertop.
- **Caution:** do not claim Grifon granite "never stains" without test data. Lead with darker colourways and care guidance.
- **Switching signal:** staining of light colours plus a frustrating warranty process.
